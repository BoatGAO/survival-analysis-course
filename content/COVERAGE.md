## Coverage conditions

- Chapter 1: all 18 case studies plus introductory data and observation concepts.
- Chapter 2: all named distribution families, formulas and conditions, practical/model checks, theoretical residual-life/discrete/mixed relationships, regression forms, and competing-risk identifiability/sensitivity ideas. Exercise topics are mapped and original practice is supplied.
- Chapter 3: all censoring designs, truncation directions, conditional likelihood construction, independence/dependence arguments, counting-process and martingale theory, estimator derivations and uncertainty, and exercise skills.
- Scope: the goal is detailed conceptual study notes. Do not infer that marking a section complete means reproducing every original proof, dataset, numerical example, citation or exercise answer.
